# Business Insight Report

## Project Context

This project analyzes a student performance dataset to identify factors that influence academic success. The analysis focuses on study habits, attendance, previous academic performance, and other student-related characteristics.

## Dataset Summary

* Total Records: 708
* Total Features: 10
* Missing Values: 0
* Duplicate Records Identified: 208

Key variables analyzed:

* Study_Hours_per_Week
* Attendance_Rate
* Past_Exam_Scores
* Final_Exam_Score
* Gender
* Extracurricular_Activities

## Analysis Approach

The dataset was inspected, cleaned, and analyzed using Python libraries including Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Descriptive statistics and visualizations were used to identify trends and relationships between variables.

## Key Findings

1. Most students scored between 50 and 65 marks in the final examination.

2. Study hours show a positive relationship with final exam performance. Students who study more tend to achieve higher scores.

3. Attendance rate is positively associated with academic performance, indicating the importance of regular participation.

4. Past exam scores have the strongest correlation with final exam scores and are the most reliable predictor of future performance.

5. The dataset contains a balanced gender distribution, reducing demographic bias in the analysis.

6. Students participating in extracurricular activities show performance patterns similar to non-participating students, suggesting that academic outcomes depend on multiple factors.

## Recommendations

1. Encourage consistent attendance among students.

2. Promote effective study planning and time management.

3. Identify students with poor past academic performance and provide additional support.

4. Use periodic assessments to monitor student progress.

5. Continue encouraging extracurricular participation while maintaining academic balance.

## Limitations

* The dataset does not include detailed socioeconomic information.
* Correlation does not imply causation.
* Additional variables may influence student performance but are not present in the dataset.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that study habits, attendance, and previous academic performance are important factors influencing final exam results. Educational institutions can improve student outcomes through targeted academic support, attendance monitoring, and effective learning strategies.
